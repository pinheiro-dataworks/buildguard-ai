Risk Priority Score — Formula
Fully documented and explainable — never a black-box executive score
RiskPriorityScore (0–100) = 45 × P(cost overrun) + 30 × P(schedule delay) + 25 × min( expected variance % / 20%, 1 )

Weights are configuration, not hard-coded model internals — see configs/business.yaml. Example — Metro Logistics Hub: 45×0.81 + 30×0.68 + 25×min(6.9/20,1) = 65.5. Sensitivity to each weight is reviewed whenever the cost matrix in business.yaml changes.

Selected operating threshold 0.16 minimizes expected business cost (860 / 1,000 projects) — about 27% lower than the conventional 0.50 default (1,182). Section 25–26 — Monitoring & Retraining Policy Tracking: MLflow (local, file-based)
BuildGuard AI is positioned as Decision Support for Construction Cost and Schedule Risk. It is never marketed as infallible, autonomous, or guaranteed — outputs are estimated risk, not certainty, and every high/critical flag requires human review before action.
Intended Use
  • Portfolio-level triage and prioritization support for cost and schedule risk review meetings
  • Early-warning signal to route projects to Planning & Cost Control for deeper investigation
  • Transparent, explainable input into executive risk conversations — always alongside engineering judgment
Out-of-Scope Use
  • Automated contract, payment, or procurement decisions without human review
  • Evaluating the performance of individual staff, crews, or suppliers
  • Projects far outside the training distribution (e.g., < $1M budget, non-standard delivery models)

Data Provenance & Privacy

Every figure in this public prototype comes from a fully synthetic construction portfolio, generated with a documented, seeded process that preserves realistic statistical relationships (e.g., CPI deterioration raising overrun risk, financial progress outpacing physical progress) without exposing any real employer, client, contractor, or supplier data. No confidential dataset is present anywhere in the public repository or its Git history.

docs/DATA_PRIVACY.md src/buildguard/data/synthetic.py
Known Limitations
  • Trained and evaluated on synthetic data — transfer to a real portfolio requires recalibration before production use
  • Procurement-delay drift (PSI 0.27) is currently above the critical threshold and under investigation
  • Infrastructure-type and large-budget (>$40M) projects carry wider, less reliable uncertainty due to smaller sample sizes
  • Feature attributions are correlational explanations of the model, not causal claims about the project
  • Probabilities are decision support only — they do not account for information outside the tracked feature set (e.g., undocumented site conditions)
