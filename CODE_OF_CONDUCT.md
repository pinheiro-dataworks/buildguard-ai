# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/)
v2.1.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy, being respectful of differing opinions and
experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and
focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include the use of sexualized language or
imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private
harassment, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainer via the contact listed on the GitHub profile
associated with this repository. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
